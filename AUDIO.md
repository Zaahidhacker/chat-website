# Private voice notes

Voice notes request microphone permission only after someone chooses Record. They use the browser’s native MediaRecorder, request 24kbps mono and support WebM/Opus, Ogg/Opus or an AAC/MP4 fallback. Recording is limited to one minute and 195KB. A browser that ignores the requested bitrate may stop sooner at the size limit. No recording or audio-processing subscription is required.

The recording dialog shows elapsed time, duration and size, with native playback, Record again, Cancel and Send controls. Backgrounding the page stops recording and releases the microphone while retaining the preview. The messaging connection’s 30-second background pause does not discard an unsent preview. Permission denial and interrupted sends have clear recovery controls. Retry sends reuse the same message UUID and the reply selected when recording began. Sending a voice reply preserves any text draft and clears the reply bar.

Every permission request, file reader, playback fetch and send callback belongs to its original dialog and account. Closing, replacing or signing out invalidates late callbacks, stops owned media tracks, aborts pending file readers, pauses the owned audio element, clears its source and revokes object URLs. A permission granted after cancellation or while the tab is hidden cannot start a recording. Closing an in-flight send cannot recall a note the server has already accepted.

Audio is fetched only when opened. Drafts and failed-send previews remain in memory; closing the dialog discards them. There is no persistent offline voice outbox. Authenticated RLS restricts stored audio to members who can see its nondeleted message, and server access expires 48 hours after sending. A cron job physically removes expired audio each minute; the chat placeholder remains. An open player also checks expiry on its timer, play and visibility changes. Recipients can retain audio already received, and messages are not end-to-end encrypted.

The existing server limits live/pending recordings to 200 (approximately 52MB at the maximum base64 size). This update changes no schema, existing messages, photos or accounts. Calling remains removed as requested.

Verification on November 3, 2026: 12 focused Chromium audio scenarios cover real synthetic-microphone recording, decoding, cleanup, denied/late permissions, overlapping recordings, hidden tabs, retry identity, reply capture, stale sends, stale account playback and expiry. Full UI tests additionally cover delivery to a second isolated client, text-draft preservation and preview retention across the messaging background pause. These use controlled transport and synthetic accounts. Physical phones, microphone hardware and Safari AAC recording remain unverified.
